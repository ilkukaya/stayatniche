## The short version

Some links on StayAtNiche are affiliate links. If you click one and then book or buy something, the partner may pay us a small commission. The price you pay is exactly the same as going to the partner directly. Links like these are marked as sponsored in our code, and buttons that lead to a partner say so nearby.

## Which partners we work with

Our affiliate links are managed through the Travelpayouts network. Depending on the page, you may see links to:

- **Hotels:** Expedia
- **Flights:** Kiwi.com
- **Tours, activities and tickets:** Klook, Tiqets
- **Airport transfers:** Welcome Pickups
- **Car hire:** Localrent
- **Luggage storage:** Radical Storage
- **Mobile data (eSIM):** Airalo
- **Travel insurance:** EKTA
- **Flight delay compensation:** AirHelp

Some other links, including many links to Booking.com and to hotels' own websites, are ordinary links that earn us nothing.

## Does this affect our recommendations?

No. We choose places because they are genuinely extraordinary and we can verify them, never because of a commercial arrangement. We do not accept payment to feature a property, and a hotel that no longer meets our standards is removed whatever its affiliate status. Our scores are editorial and are not guest reviews.

## Prices

Prices on StayAtNiche are typical nightly ranges from our research, shown in US dollars. They are a guide only. The live price for your dates is always the one on the booking site.
