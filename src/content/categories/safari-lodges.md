A safari lodge at dawn: the savanna turns gold, a pride of lions moves through the grass two hundred metres away, and your guide is whispering quietly into the radio. By 8am you're back at camp for a full breakfast. By 11am, you're in your private plunge pool watching elephants at the waterhole.

## Why Choose a Safari Lodge?

The Big Five in their natural habitat. Landscapes that dwarf everything you've ever seen. The absolute silence of the African night, broken only by distant hyena calls. Safari lodges provide access to the continent's greatest wildlife spectacles while maintaining extraordinary comfort — some properties rival the world's finest hotels for cuisine, design, and service.

## Top Safari Destinations

Kenya's Maasai Mara hosts the Great Migration — 1.5 million wildebeest crossing the Mara River — from July to October. Tanzania's Serengeti offers year-round big cat sightings. Botswana's Okavango Delta is the world's most extraordinary waterway, best explored by mokoro canoe from a permanent tented camp. South Africa's Kruger provides the most accessible safari experience, with world-class lodges along its private concessions.
