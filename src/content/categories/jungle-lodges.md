Waking to the howl of howler monkeys. Watching toucans from your breakfast table. Falling asleep to the percussion of a tropical rainstorm on a palm-leaf roof. Jungle lodges deliver an immersion in nature that no safari or beach holiday can replicate.

## Why Choose a Jungle Lodge?

Biodiversity beyond imagination — the world's rainforests contain more species per square kilometre than any other ecosystem. The best jungle lodges are positioned for maximum wildlife encounters: rivers for caimans and capybaras, forest trails for jaguars and tapirs, canopies for sloths and toucans.

## Top Jungle Destinations

Costa Rica leads the world in eco-lodge excellence, with properties ranging from carbon-neutral tented camps to glass-and-timber lodges with private plunge pools. Borneo offers the extraordinary experience of orangutan tracking from your lodge doorstep. The Amazon — accessible from Peru, Brazil, or Colombia — represents the ultimate jungle frontier.
