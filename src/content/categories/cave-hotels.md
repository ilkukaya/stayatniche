Carved from the living rock over centuries, cave hotels invite you to sleep inside the earth itself. These extraordinary stays maintain a natural temperature year-round and offer a sense of timelessness that no conventional hotel can match.

## Why Choose a Cave Hotel?

Cave hotels are naturally temperature-regulated, staying cool in summer and warm in winter. They're also extraordinarily atmospheric — there's nothing quite like waking up in a space that has been carved into rock over thousands of years.

## Top Cave Hotel Destinations

Cappadocia in Turkey is the undisputed king of cave hotels, with hundreds of properties carved into ancient volcanic tufa. Santorini in Greece offers another take with cliff-side cave suites overlooking the caldera.
