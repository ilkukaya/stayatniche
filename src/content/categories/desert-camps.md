The desert is the earth stripped bare — no trees, no noise, no distractions. Just sand, silence, and a sky so dense with stars it looks painted. Luxury desert camps harness this elemental beauty and add improbable comfort: silk-lined tents, gourmet dinners cooked over open fire, and private astronomical viewings.

## Why Choose a Desert Camp?

The Milky Way visible to the naked eye. Silence so complete you can hear your own heartbeat. Temperatures that plummet dramatically after sunset, making a warm camp fire feel essential. Desert camps offer a sensory reset that no other accommodation type can match.

## Top Desert Destinations

Morocco's Sahara — particularly the Erg Chebbi dunes near Merzouga — is the classic choice, with camel treks to camp a rite of passage. Jordan's Wadi Rum offers a Mars-like landscape of red rock and vast silence. Oman's Wahiba Sands and the UAE's Liwa oasis deliver luxury Arabian Desert experiences.
