Centuries of history surround you when you stay in a castle hotel. These magnificent properties — once home to kings, knights, and nobles — have been carefully restored to offer every modern comfort while preserving their extraordinary historic character.

## Why Choose a Castle Hotel?

Staying in a castle isn't just accommodation — it's time travel. You walk the same corridors as historical figures, dine in grand banquet halls, and sleep in rooms that have witnessed centuries of history.

## Castle Hotel Experiences

Many castle hotels offer unique experiences beyond just sleeping: falconry, horse riding, whiskey tastings in ancient cellars, and traditional afternoon teas. Ireland and Scotland lead the world in castle hotel hospitality.
