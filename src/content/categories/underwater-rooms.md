The world's rarest accommodation type, underwater hotel rooms place you in direct contact with the ocean's spectacular marine life. Fall asleep watching reef fish drift past your bedroom window; wake to manta rays gliding overhead.

## Why Choose an Underwater Room?

There are fewer than a dozen true underwater hotel rooms in the entire world, making this the ultimate bucket-list stay. The experience is incomparable — falling asleep surrounded by the ocean, with marine life as your constant companion.

## What Makes Them Special

These rooms require extraordinary engineering to create — reinforced glass panels, specialized lighting that doesn't disturb marine ecosystems, and careful environmental management. The result is a stay that feels genuinely otherworldly.
