Every November, teams of ice architects descend on subarctic Sweden, Finland, and Norway with chainsaws and shovels. By December, a palace of ice stands ready for guests — sleeping rooms sculpted by artists, bars serving cocktails in ice glasses, and corridors lined with intricate carvings. By April, it returns to the river.

## Why Choose an Ice Hotel?

Ice hotels exist at the intersection of art installation and architecture. No two are identical — each year brings new artists, new themes, new ice sculptures. Sleeping at -5°C in a reindeer-skin sleeping bag is a genuinely extraordinary experience. Most properties also offer warm accommodation options for those who want the experience without the cold night.

## The Essential Experience

The world's first and most famous is the ICEHOTEL in Jukkasjärvi, Sweden, established in 1990. Finland's Kemi SnowCastle and Norway's Sorrisniva Igloo Hotel offer alternative takes. Canada's Hôtel de Glace in Quebec City brings the experience to North America.
