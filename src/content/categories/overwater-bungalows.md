The overwater bungalow is perhaps the most photographed hotel concept in the world — and for good reason. Standing at the end of a timber walkway, surrounded entirely by ocean, you have complete privacy combined with complete immersion in one of the planet's most beautiful environments.

## Why Choose an Overwater Bungalow?

The glass floor panels showing tropical fish below your living room. The wooden staircase descending directly into a lagoon so clear you can see the sandy bottom at 4 metres depth. Waking to the sound of water on all sides. Overwater bungalows deliver a sensory experience unlike any land-based accommodation.

## Top Destinations

The Maldives has arguably overtaken French Polynesia as the world's overwater bungalow capital, with hundreds of properties across 26 atolls offering everything from accessible mid-range options to extraordinary ultra-luxury villas with private pools. Bora Bora remains the romantic ideal, with Mount Otemanu rising dramatically from the lagoon. The Philippines, Thailand, and Fiji offer more accessible price points without sacrificing the essential experience.
