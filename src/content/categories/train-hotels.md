Train hotels blur the line between transportation and destination. Aboard these rolling palaces, the journey itself becomes a once-in-a-lifetime experience — dining in mahogany-panelled restaurant cars, watching landscapes roll past your private cabin window, and sleeping to the gentle rhythm of the rails.

## The Golden Age of Rail, Reimagined

Legendary trains like the Venice Simplon-Orient-Express have been lovingly restored to their 1920s glory, complete with art deco marquetry, polished brass fittings, and white-glove service. Other train hotels are carved into converted Victorian-era carriages repurposed as static luxury accommodation in scenic locations.

## What to Expect

Cabins range from intimate sleepers with fold-down berths to grand suites with private sitting rooms. Fine dining, cocktail bars, and observation cars are standard on the premier services.
