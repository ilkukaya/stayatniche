Lighthouse hotels offer a stay unlike any other — perched on rocky headlands, sandy spits, or remote islands, these converted maritime structures combine historical charm with breathtaking coastal scenery.

## The Lighthouse Experience

Sleeping in a lighthouse means waking to ocean views from every window, the rhythm of waves below, and sunsets that stretch across unbroken horizons. Many retain their original Fresnel lenses, spiral staircases, and keeper's quarters, transformed into atmospheric guest rooms.

## What to Expect

Most lighthouse hotels are small and intimate — often just a few rooms within the keeper's cottage or tower itself. Expect spiral staircases, circular rooms, and panoramic lantern room views that no conventional hotel can match.
