There's something primal about sleeping on the edge — the vast open air below, the stars above, and the sense that you're suspended between earth and sky. Cliffside hotels take the most dramatic locations on the planet and plant a room right at the precipice.

## Why Choose a Cliffside Hotel?

The view is everything. Waking up to a sunrise over a sheer cliff drop — whether it's the turquoise Aegean, the Amalfi Coast, or a Norwegian fjord — is one of travel's most enduring thrills. These stays also tend to be architecturally ambitious: when you're building on a cliff, ordinary won't do.

## Top Destinations

The Amalfi Coast delivers the quintessential cliffside experience, with villages clinging to vertiginous limestone faces above the Mediterranean. Santorini's caldera-edge infinity pools define aspirational travel imagery. In Asia, hotels built into the limestone karst of Halong Bay and Phang Nga Bay offer their own dramatic take on elevation.
