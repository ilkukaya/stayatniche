Sleeping in a treehouse is one of those childhood dreams that, if you choose the right property, translates into an extraordinary adult experience. The best treehouse hotels combine architectural ingenuity, deep immersion in nature, and a level of comfort that makes you forget you're suspended 10 metres above the forest floor.

We've selected the 10 finest examples from across the globe.


Sweden's legendary Treehotel is the gold standard. Seven architect-designed rooms, including the iconic Mirrored Cube and the Bird's Nest, hang suspended in the pines outside Harads. In winter, fall asleep hoping for the Northern Lights. In summer, the midnight sun lights the forest until late.

**Best for:** Architecture lovers, aurora hunters, design enthusiasts
**Price from:** $300/night

Nestled in the Monteverde Cloud Forest, these luxury treehouse suites offer exceptional access to Costa Rica's extraordinary biodiversity. Wake to howler monkeys, hummingbirds, and resplendent quetzals directly from your private deck.


Beyond the novelty, treehouse stays offer something rare in modern travel: genuine connection with the natural world. You fall asleep to wind moving through branches. You wake to birdsong rather than alarm clocks. The forest becomes your living room.

The best treehouse hotels are also leaders in sustainable tourism: minimal footprint, maximum experience.
