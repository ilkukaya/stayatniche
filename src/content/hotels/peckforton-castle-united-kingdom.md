Peckforton Castle was built in 1851 to recreate a medieval fortress, and it still reads as one: a Grade I listed Victorian Gothic stronghold in the Cheshire countryside, described by its owners as the only intact medieval-style castle in England. Today it works as a hotel, restaurant and events venue, and overnight guests sleep inside the walls rather than in a modern annexe.

## The rooms

There are 48 individually appointed bedrooms, each with its own character rather than a uniform template. The most dramatic are the Tower Suites, set in the castle's iconic towers. For larger parties there is also secluded group accommodation with hot tubs, a separate option for private groups.

## The setting

The castle stands on a 48-acre estate of woodland walks and formal gardens. On a clear day the elevated position gives panoramic views stretching to the Welsh mountains. The grounds are a large part of the appeal, and guests can walk them freely during a stay. The castle has also been used as a location for film and television productions.

## Eating and staying

The 1851 Restaurant is an award-winning fine dining room open to both residents and non-residents, so dinner can be part of the stay. WiFi, parking and access to the castle grounds are included with every castle stay. A year-round programme of ticketed events runs through the calendar, and the Land Rover Experience is offered on the estate.

## Getting there

Peckforton sits about 20 minutes by car from Chester city centre, which makes it an easy base for the city and for the Cheshire countryside. It is a popular wedding venue, so weekends can be busy.

If you want the towers, ask for a Tower Suite when you book.
