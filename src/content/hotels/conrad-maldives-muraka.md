Muraka — meaning "coral" in Dhivehi — is the singular most extraordinary hotel room on the planet. Launched in 2019 after five years of development, it required engineering solutions that had never been attempted in hospitality before.

## The Experience

The suite occupies two levels. The upper level features a master bedroom, living room, and outdoor deck at water level, with sweeping views across the Indian Ocean. A spiral staircase descends to the lower level — a curved bedroom and bathroom built 5 meters below the ocean's surface, surrounded entirely by curved acrylic panels offering a 180-degree view of the living reef.

Guests fall asleep watching reef sharks, rays, and thousands of tropical fish drift past. The lighting is carefully calibrated not to disturb the marine ecosystem while still providing enough illumination for the extraordinary view.

## The Service

Muraka comes with a complete private staff: a personal butler, dedicated chef, and dive instructor. The rate includes unlimited access to Conrad's world-class diving facilities and spa, as well as curated experiences from twilight dolphin cruises to private sandbank picnics.

## Sustainability

Conrad Maldives is deeply committed to reef conservation, and Muraka's construction was designed to minimize environmental impact. The property participates in active coral restoration programs, and guests can participate in reef monitoring during their stay.
