Ottoman Cave Suites is described by its own site as a cosy, comfortable small rock hotel in an area with easy access to Göreme's centre and many of Cappadocia's sights. The hotel states it has 13 rooms in total: eight carved into rock and five standard stone rooms. So most of the stay is cave accommodation, but a minority of rooms are not.

## The rooms

The rooms are grouped on the website. The standard stone rooms are 301, 302, 304 and 305, while the standard rock-carved rooms include 101, 102 and 104. Deluxe rooms are 103, 203 and 204, and there are suites 201 and 303, plus a King Suite, 202. The hotel says five of its rock-carved suites have views of Uçhisar Castle, and one standard stone suite has views on both sides.

## Views and breakfast

The hotel advertises panoramic views over the valleys, the mountains and the Erciyes volcano. Breakfast can be taken on the terrace with a view of the fairy chimneys. The site also mentions a restaurant, though it gives no menu or opening hours in the material we reviewed.

## Practical notes

The listed address is Avcılar Mahallesi, Okul Sokak No: 6, 50180 Göreme. The website lists the hotel as brand new, so it is a recent opening rather than a long-established house. Prices were not available to us, so rates vary. A pool and spa are not mentioned.

This is a decent choice if you want a cave room in Göreme at a hotel small enough to feel personal. Request a rock-carved room explicitly when booking.
