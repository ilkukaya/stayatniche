Museum Hotel in Uçhisar is the crown jewel of Cappadocia's extraordinary cave hotel scene. Perched at the highest point of the region, it commands views across the entire fairy chimney landscape that feel almost unreal.


Each of Museum Hotel's 30 suites is genuinely one-of-a-kind. The hotel's founder spent decades collecting Anatolian antiques, and these pieces, carpets, ceramics, textiles, and furniture, fill every room. Many suites feature private terraces where you can watch the dawn hot air balloons rise over the valley.


The Lil'a Restaurant consistently ranks among Turkey's finest, serving elevated versions of Anatolian cuisine in a cave dining room of extraordinary beauty. The spa, also carved into the rock, offers traditional Turkish hammam treatments alongside modern therapies.


The hotel's concierge team excels at organizing the essential Cappadocia experiences: sunrise hot air balloon flights over the fairy chimneys, guided walks through the underground cities, and off-the-beaten-track valley hikes that avoid the crowds.
