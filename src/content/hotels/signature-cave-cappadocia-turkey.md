Signature Cave Cappadocia Trademark Collection by Wyndham is a branded boutique hotel that builds its identity around cave suites. The hotel's own wording is that guests are "immersed" in Cappadocia's tranquillity in luxurious cave suites, where history meets modern comfort. It is the one cave hotel in this batch that sits under an international hotel group's banner.

## The suites

The site's room pages show the style. The Signature Pool Suite, with one double bed, has direct access to the pool area and a private outdoor sitting area. The in-room features it lists are a laptop-sized safe, a desk, prayer mats, a clothes hamper, a sitting area and air conditioning. Not every suite is described in the material we reviewed, so the pool suite should not be taken as typical of the whole hotel. Room counts are not published.

## The setting

The hotel describes itself as set among Cappadocia's fairy chimneys and ancient rock formations, with suites that each offer panoramic views of the landscape. Its stated promise is a mix of cultural heritage and luxurious comfort. The site is available in Turkish and English and has an area-guide section.

## Practical notes

Prices were not available to us, so rates vary. Check-in and check-out times, breakfast arrangements and any spa or restaurant are not stated in the pages we reviewed, so confirm them with the hotel. Because it operates within a hotel group, expect more standardised booking and policies than at the family-run cave houses nearby.

The property is a good fit for travellers who want a cave stay with the familiarity of a branded hotel, and for those who want a pool tied directly to their room. If you prefer a small family-run house with a personal story, other entries in this guide may suit you better.
