Aldourie Castle is not a hotel in the usual sense. It is a baronial castle on the shores of Loch Ness, run by WildLand, and it is booked for exclusive use: you take the whole house. The castle sleeps 24 guests across 12 en-suite bedrooms, so it suits a group or a family gathering rather than a couple looking for a single room.

## The house

The site describes the castle as rooted in the classical heritage of a Scottish country house, with a contemporary Danish design influence. The rooms are described as elegant, weaving together comfort and character, with a team looking after guests. The castle is fully staffed and includes daily housekeeping.

## Food

Food is a central part of the stay. All meals are prepared by the in-house team of chefs, with much of the produce grown in the castle's walled garden or sourced from local suppliers, the estates or foraged. The site names the Dining Room for formal banquets, the Boathouse for relaxed lunches, the drawing room for afternoon tea and the Laird's Room for breakfasts.

## Outdoors and wellbeing

Guests can use the Sauna Yurt among the trees by Loch Ness, and the Steadings house a wellness space. Full concierge and trip planning are available from the point of booking.

## Practical notes

The castle is in the Highlands near Inverness. It is a strong choice for celebrations and group holidays; it is not set up for solo overnight stays.
