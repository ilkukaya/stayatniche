The Maldives has no shortage of exceptional overwater villa resorts, but Four Seasons Landaa Giraavaru earns its place at the very top of that competitive field through a combination of factors that extends well beyond the quality of its accommodation, remarkable as that accommodation is. The resort occupies a clear island in Baa Atoll, designated as a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve and recognised as one of the richest marine environments in the Indian Ocean. The manta ray aggregations at the nearby Hanifaru Bay, where hundreds of reef mantas feed in a choreographed spiral that has no equivalent anywhere in the world, are among the most extraordinary wildlife spectacles available to non-scientific observers anywhere on Earth.

The resort's overwater bungalows extend on wooden walkways above a lagoon of extraordinary colour and clarity. Each villa has a private plunge pool on its deck, steps descending directly to the lagoon for swimming, and glass floor panels that make visible the reef fish, rays, and occasional reef sharks that patrol the shallow water below. The architecture is tropical contemporary, high thatched ceilings, bleached timber, stone and glass, and the sense of space is genuine: these are not the cramped overwater rooms of older resort designs but generous living environments where the view of the lagoon is ever-present.

The Marine Discovery Centre is what elevates Landaa Giraavaru beyond the best of its competitors. The resident team of marine biologists conducts ongoing research on the Baa Atoll ecosystem and runs a programme of guest activities, snorkelling excursions to Hanifaru Bay during manta season (June to November), reef restoration projects, coral propagation workshops, and evening lectures on Indian Ocean marine ecology, that transform a beach holiday into an intellectually serious engagement with one of the world's most threatened marine ecosystems. Guests leave knowing more about the Maldives than they arrived with, which is a very rare quality in luxury resort hospitality.

The culinary offer is Four Seasons standard throughout: the range of restaurants covers contemporary Maldivian, Japanese, Mediterranean, and casual Indian Ocean fare, with a wine programme and cocktail menu that could stand comparison with the best hotel restaurants in the world's capital cities. The spa, built over the water, offers treatments that draw on traditional Maldivian healing practices alongside contemporary wellness therapies. It is, taken in its entirety, a resort that has understood that true luxury in the twenty-first century must have substance as well as beauty.
