The French Alps have long been synonymous with luxury ski hospitality, but L'Aigle des Neiges, The Snow Eagle, offers something that the grand chalets and five-star ski lodges of the Tarentaise valley cannot match: the experience of sleeping inside a transparent dome at altitude, with the entire panorama of the Écrins massif visible from your bed and the Milky Way blazing overhead in air so clear and cold it seems to crackle.

The property sits on the southern edge of Les Deux Alpes, a resort known for its exceptional glacier skiing and its access to one of the highest ski domains in Europe. The bubble suites are positioned on a terrace that commands unobstructed views across the Romanche valley and up toward the 3,568-metre summit of La Muzelle, a view that is striking in daylight and almost surreally beautiful under a full Alpine moon.

At 1,650 metres above sea level, the air at Les Deux Alpes is measurably cleaner and drier than the valleys below, and this translates directly into stargazing conditions of real quality. The Alpine climate means clear nights are frequent, particularly in the shoulder seasons of late autumn and early spring when the ski domain is open but crowds are thin. Guests who choose the bubble accommodation report that lying in the heated dome while fresh snow falls silently outside, the flakes catching the light before disappearing into the dark, is among the most memorably beautiful experiences they have encountered anywhere in Europe.

Days revolve around one of France's premier ski domains: 225 kilometres of marked runs, a glacier that allows summer skiing into July, and a terrain park that attracts freestyle skiers from across the continent. The resort's vertical drop of more than 2,100 metres means runs of real length and character, and the snow quality on the upper glacier is reliably excellent throughout the season.

After skiing, the lodge restaurant serves the Savoyard classics, tartiflette, fondue, raclette, with a wine list that extends respectfully into the Savoie and Rhône appellations. It is a thoroughly French experience, combining the specific pleasures of Alpine sport with the unusual intimacy of sleeping beneath the mountain sky.
