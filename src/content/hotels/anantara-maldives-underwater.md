Anantara Kihavah Maldives Villas has constructed around its extraordinary Baa Atoll setting an experience architecture of exceptional ambition. The resort organises its main experiences around four elements, FIRE (the grill restaurant), SKY (the observatory), ICE (the wine cellar), and SEA (the underwater restaurant), and it is the last of these that has established the property's international reputation and made it one of the most sought-after dining reservations in the Indian Ocean.

SEA is a genuine underwater restaurant: circular in form, its transparent walls and ceiling immersed five metres below the surface of the Maldivian ocean. The 270-degree panoramic glass panels reveal a living coral reef in full functioning health, reef fish in constant motion, sea turtles cruising past with apparent indifference to the candlelit dinner happening three metres from their flippers, and at night, the extraordinary spectacle of the reef illuminated by concealed lighting while the water above remains dark. It is among the most theatrically spectacular dining environments in the world, and the kitchen team works hard to ensure the food, contemporary Maldivian and international cuisine built on the finest available Indian Ocean ingredients, is worthy of the setting.

The overwater villas extend this underwater philosophy into the accommodation. Floors in the villa living areas include panels of clear glass positioned over living reef sections, so that guests can observe the reef fish, invertebrates, and occasional rays that inhabit the coral directly below. This is a different experience from the underwater rooms of Jules' Undersea Lodge or the Manta Resort, guests are above rather than among the marine life, but the intimacy is genuine: sitting in a floor-level bath positioned over a glass panel, watching a hawksbill turtle feed on coral two metres below, is a moment of connection with the marine world that no aquarium can replicate.

The Baa Atoll location, the same UNESCO Biosphere Reserve that houses the Four Seasons Landaa Giraavaru, provides access to Hanifaru Bay and its legendary manta ray aggregations during the southwest monsoon season. The resort's resident marine biologist programme offers reef monitoring dives, manta identification sessions, and night snorkelling in the lagoon, extending the underwater experience beyond the built environment and into the wild reef that surrounds the island. Butler service, a Spa Over Water built on stilts above the reef, and the resort's comprehensive water sports programme complete an offer that is among the most complete in the Maldives.
