Charming Cave Cappadocia Hotel is a guesthouse in Göreme that says its rooms are carved from natural rock. It has been in service for over ten years, and describes fifteen styled rooms offering comfortable and relaxed accommodation. The site mixes cave rooms with stone rooms, so a request for a cave room is worth making when you book.

## The rooms

The website lists several types. Standard Cave Rooms are five economical but uniquely designed cave rooms. There is a Standard Cave Triple Suite with three beds, aimed at families. Standard Stone Rooms and Deluxe Stone Rooms are built-stone rooms with Cappadocian architecture. The Cave Suite is a cave-style room combining authentic architecture with modern comfort. Other types are Suite Rooms, Junior Suites and a Superior Double for two.

## Facilities

Breakfast is served from 7:30 to 10:00. The hotel offers free high-speed internet, free parking on the street, and transfers from Nevşehir and Kayseri airports. The terrace has views of the hot air balloons.

## Practical notes

The listed address is Aydınlı Orta Mahalle, Kevenci Sk. No:16-1, 50180 Göreme. Prices were not available to us, so rates vary. A pool or spa is not mentioned. Check-in and check-out times are not stated in the pages we reviewed.

This is a good fit for travellers who want a straightforward cave room in Göreme at a likely modest price, and for families who can use the triple suite. As with most mixed hotels, specify the cave room category when you reserve, and confirm bed types for the triple suite if you are travelling as a family.
