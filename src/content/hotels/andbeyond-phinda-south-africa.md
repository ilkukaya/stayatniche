The story of Phinda begins as a conservation story. In the late 1980s, what is now one of Africa's most celebrated private reserves was exhausted farmland — overgrazed, wildlife-depleted, and economically marginal. &Beyond acquired the land in 1990 and embarked on one of the continent's most ambitious private wildlife restoration programmes: fences removed, land rested, and animals reintroduced in a carefully sequenced programme that rebuilt an entire ecosystem from the ground up. Today, Phinda is home to all of the Big Five, to cheetah at a density that makes reliable sightings almost routine, to the only sand forest ecosystem in Africa, and to wildlife populations that have recovered beyond what the reserve's founders dared to project.

Seven distinct habitat types coexist within the reserve's boundaries: the sand forest, found nowhere else on the continent and home to secretive forest cats and rare Rudd's apalis; savanna woodland; wetland; riverine forest; open plains; rocky hillside; and the transition zones between them where ecological richness is at its highest. This variety means that each game drive passes through genuinely different landscapes with different species assemblages — the photographic and natural history interest doesn't plateau after the first day, as it can at reserves with a single dominant habitat.

The cheetah experience at Phinda is exceptional by any standard. The open plains sections of the reserve support a healthy resident population, and the rangers' knowledge of individual animals and territories makes sightings — including hunts — consistently available. Black and white rhino coexist on the same reserve, a conservation achievement that remains rare in African safari. The birdlife across the seven habitats encompasses over 400 species, including several forest endemics and near-endemics that attract dedicated birders from around the world.

&Beyond operates six camps within the reserve, from the dramatic Forest Lodge — its suites suspended above a sand forest on stilts, windows framing the canopy at tree level — to the Rock Lodge's extraordinary bouldered hillside setting, and the classic-style Vlei Lodge positioned above a seasonal wetland. At the coast, the Ocean Lodge offers a genuinely different experience: a luxury beach property within reach of the reserve, enabling a combination itinerary that moves from bush game drives in the morning to Indian Ocean swimming in the afternoon.

The community conservation work that &Beyond pioneered at Phinda — partnerships with surrounding communities that provide employment, education, and direct financial benefits from the reserve's success — has become a model for sustainable African safari development. Staying here directly supports one of the continent's most genuinely effective conservation and community benefit programmes.
