Peristyle Cave Cappadocia is a small hotel high in Uçhisar, beneath the castle that gives the village its skyline. The hotel states that it has eight rooms in total: five caves and three stone rooms. So this is a stay where most, though not all, of the rooms are carved into the rock.

## The rooms

The rooms are named after the planets: Uranus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Neptune, Mercury, Earth and Venus. The website lists them as Rooms and Suites, but does not spell out which names are the cave rooms and which are the stone ones, so ask when booking if you specifically want a cave room. Room sizes and bed types are not published in the material we reviewed.

## The setting

The hotel describes itself as being "in the skies of Uçhisar Castle" with a bird's-eye view over Cappadocia. Its headline claim is that on a morning terrace you can almost touch the hot air balloons. Güvercinlik Valley, a walking valley, is in the immediate vicinity, and the hotel says museums and ruins can be reached on foot, on daily tours, or by activity vehicles such as horse and ATV rides.

## Food and drink

Breakfast is described as mixed and organic, prepared fresh, and can be taken on the terrace or in the room. The hotel also offers Cappadocian wines to taste on the terrace or in the lounge.

## Activities

The hotel lists a balloon tour, an ATV tour, a horse-riding tour and a daily tour on its activities page.

## Practical notes

The listed address is Asagi Gedik Sokak No:37, 50240 Uçhisar, Nevşehir. Prices and check-in times are not stated, so rates vary and you should confirm them directly. With only eight rooms, it fills early in balloon season.
