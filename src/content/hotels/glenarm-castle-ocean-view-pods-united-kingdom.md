The Ocean View Pods are a small group of glamping pods on the Glenarm Castle estate in County Antrim, on the coast of Northern Ireland. They are not rooms inside the castle: the stay is in a pod, with the castle estate as the setting. Each pod is individually decorated to reflect the surrounding landscape, with an expansive glazed entrance, a private shower room and a seating area, and every one looks out across Glenarm Bay.

## The pods

Each pod sleeps up to two adults, two teens or children and one infant in a travel cot. It has a double bed with hotel-quality linen and integrated bunk beds, which suit guests up to 5ft 5in because they are smaller than a standard single. Facilities include Wi-Fi, USB charging points, a mini-fridge, microwave, kettle and hairdryer, plus thermostat-controlled heating. The glass frontage has no curtains, so eye masks are provided. Every pod has a private fire pit.

Two pods, Bay View and Tully, have an eco-friendly wood-fired hot tub. It is heated by a stove, which takes roughly 1.5 to 2.5 hours to warm up, so plan your arrival accordingly. One pod is dog friendly.

## Spa and extras

Guests can also book the Ocean View Retreat, a private hillside spa overlooking the glen and coast. Sessions include a covered jacuzzi, a sauna and a cold plunge barrel. Extras include s'mores for the fire pit, breakfast in bed, and early check-in or late check-out, subject to availability.

Check-in is from 3pm to 9pm and check-out is by 10am. There is a separate stay in a 19th-century gatehouse above the castle wall, so ask if you want something in the castle's own walls.
